GARY COMMANDS
=============
Say "Gary" in the sentence so he knows it's for him (mic or typed in the control page).
Words in [brackets] are optional. Most commands match loosely, so close wording works too.


MOVING AROUND / STAYING STILL  (this is the stationary toggle)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Stationary ON:
  "Gary, stay put"                  "Gary, stay still"        "Gary, don't move"
  "Gary, stop moving"               "Gary, stand still"       "Gary, stay stationary"
  "Gary, stay at the bottom"        (stays put, close to the camera)
  "Gary, stay put on the left"      / "on the right" / "in the middle" / "in the back"

Moving again (pick how much):
  "Gary, wander around"             small: paces around his spot (add "at the bottom", "on the left" etc.)
  "Gary, free roam"                 big: walks all over the stage and uses props
  "Gary, walk on mode"              hides until he has something to say

He remembers the last setting after a restart. In stay mode he doesn't use the prop
activities (DJ deck, couch, bed, arcade...); those only happen in wander/free roam.
You can also switch these and turn single activities on/off on the control page
(http://localhost:7777/control, card "What the 3D sidekick does on his own").


SCREEN LAYOUTS (he keeps walking around, but only in one part of the screen)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Gary, bottom strip"  /  "Gary, walk along the bottom"  /  "Gary, only go at the bottom"
                                    small, walks left and right along the bottom edge
  "Gary, top strip"  /  "Gary, head to the back"        small line way in the back, near the top
  "Gary, left side"  /  "Gary, stick to the left side"  /  same with right
                                    a column down that side, walking front and back
  "Gary, go to the bottom left corner"  /  "Gary, right corner mode"
                                    a small patch in that bottom corner
Back to normal with "Gary, free roam" / "wander around" / "stay put" / "walk on mode".
In a layout he's drawn smaller (the Size slider still scales him on top of that), skips the
prop activities (DJ deck, couch, bed...) so the screen stays clear, and stops where he is to talk.
"Stay at the bottom" is still stay put (standing still, close to the camera).
Buttons for all six are in the OBS dock (Movement card) and on the control page.

QUIET / PAUSE
-------------
  "Gary, hold that thought" / "hang on" / "wait" / "stop" / "stop it"   cuts him off mid-line
  (while he's talking. Stop it only cuts him off; it doesn't mute him)
  "Gary, go ahead" / "what were you saying"                  finishes the line you cut off
  "Gary, stop talking for 15 minutes"                        muted for that long (max 60)
  "Gary, be quiet" / "shut up" / "shush"                     muted until you call him back
  "Gary, take five" / "take ten"                             5 / 10 minutes
  "Gary, go away for 10 minutes"                             muted, but stays on screen doing idle stuff
  "Gary, you can talk again" / "come back" / "unmute"        ends the mute
  Saying "Gary" to him while he's muted also brings him back.
While muted he ignores chat but still reacts to follows, subs, bits, raids and redeems.


DEAFEN (he can't hear your mic at all)
--------------------------------------
  Big red "Deafen Gary" button at the top of the OBS dock (click again to let him hear).
  Dock / control page also have 5, 15 and 30 minute buttons.
  "Gary, cover your ears" / "earmuffs" / "plug your ears"    deafens him for 15 minutes
  "Gary, cover your ears for 30 minutes"                     that long (max 4 hours)
  While deafened nothing you say is turned into text, logged or remembered. Chat still reaches him.
  He can't hear you ask him to listen again: use the dock button, the control page, or a Streamer.bot
  hotkey action that fetches  http://localhost:7777/api/deafen?on=toggle
  BRB scenes deafen him on their own and undo it when you leave them.
  It stays on after a restart or crash until you turn it off.
  "Forget 10m" (dock) / "Forget last 10 min" (control page)   erases the last 10 minutes of what was
                                                             said, before he learns anything from it


GARY GROWING UP (his mission)
-----------------------------
He starts out knowing almost nothing: not what the stream is, not what chat is ("the Voices"), just
that you're dum. He learns from what happens on stream, grows through 4 stages (Lost, Noticing, the
big realization that he's ON a stream, Co-host) based on hours streamed and how much he remembers,
and one day asks if he can run the stream himself. Every now and then (about every 20-30 minutes, at
a quiet moment) he asks you a curious question about the stream, life or big ideas. Your next line is
saved as what you taught him, and he brings it up later.
  "Gary, stop talking about the dumpster"                    he drops that topic for good
  "Gary, read your journal"                                  reads his diary entry from last stream
  Dock card "Gary's mission": stage and progress, Let him ask, Freeze, Stage - / Stage +.
  Control page: the same, plus "Realize he's on stream now" and a Memory panel where you can see and
  delete anything he remembers.


VOICE EFFECTS (on you, through Streamer.bot)
--------------------------------------------
  "Gary, robot voice me" / "give me the helium voice" / "make me deep voice"
  Voices: robot, helium, deep, echo, alien. The Streamer.bot action needs the word voice in its name
  (like ROBOT VOICE), so a Robot Dance Clip action is never used as a voice.
  "Gary, random voice please"                                he picks one
  "Gary, normal voice" / "turn off the voice"                back to normal
He sometimes puts one on you by himself (every 20+ minutes at most).


STREAM FX
---------
  "Gary, put me on acid" / "acid trip me"                    FX - DUM ON ACID
  "Gary, make me drunk"                                      FX - MAKE DUM DRUNK
  (Both actions need to be in the Streamer.bot "Sidekick" group.)


3D REACTIONS
------------
  "Gary, hype it up" / "celebrate" / "party time" / "Gary, let's go"    hype party
  "Gary, rip" / "press F" / "I died" / "pay respects"                   rip animation
Chat can set these off too: hype emotes / LETS GO spam, or a wave of F's.


GAMES
-----
  "Gary, start a poll: pizza or tacos or sushi"              poll with your options
  "Gary, start a poll about snacks"                          he makes one up
  "Gary, end the poll"
  "Gary, trivia time"  /  "Gary, trivia about movies"  /  "Gary, trivia about movies, five rounds"  /  "Gary, stop trivia"
  (rounds or questions: a number or one to ten in words; the words trivia night alone do not start it)
  "Gary, start a prediction: will I beat the boss"
  "Gary, lock the prediction" / "Gary, cancel the prediction"
  "Gary, the answer is yes" / "Gary, option 2 won"           pays out the prediction
  (the answer must be one of the options; an answer like no idea does nothing)
  "Gary, who's winning" / "leaderboard"                      points leaderboard
Chat: types 1/2/3 or !vote 2 for polls, a/b/c/d for trivia, !pick <name> for predictions and races.


MARBLE RACES
------------
  "Gary, the marble race is starting"  /  "Gary, start a race"       (adding soon to the end does not start one)
  "Gary, Kevin won"  /  "Gary, the winner is Kevin"               (only while a race is running)
  "Gary, the race is over"


CLIPS, VISION, RECAP
--------------------
  "Gary, clip that"                                          runs the Clip It action and saves the moment
  (the Streamer.bot action needs the word clip in its name)
  "Gary, look at this" / "what do you see" / "watch this" / "see this on screen"
                                                             he looks at the stream and reacts
                                                             (if vision is off or OBS is down, he says so)
  "Gary, recap the stream" / "wrap it up"                    end-of-stream recap


SCENES (automatic, no command)
------------------------------
OBS scene names change his mode: starting, brb, chatting, gaming, marble, chill, party, ending.
If a name fits more than one, the first in features.toml [scene_modes] wins: a scene called Marble Starting Soon is marble.
Scenes with "news" or "weather" as a word in the name make him the DUMSTREAM News co-anchor / Weather guy.
