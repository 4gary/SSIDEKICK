Sidekick control

Stay put: stands still. Wander: paces around his spot. Free roam: walks the whole screen, near and far. Walk on: hidden until he has something to say.
Tick what he's allowed to do when nobody is talking to him. "Do it" makes him do it right now. He drops anything the moment he has something to say. Props only show up while he walks over to use them. The DJ deck, couch, bed, arcade, desk and butterfly need Wander or Free roam.
