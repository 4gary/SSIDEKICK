Stay put: stands still. Wander: paces around his spot. Free roam: walks the whole screen, near and far. Walk on: hidden until he has something to say. Screen layouts (second row): he walks around but only in that part of the screen, a bit smaller, and skips the prop activities.
Tick what he's allowed to do when nobody is talking to him. "Do it" makes him do it right now. He drops anything the moment he has something to say. Props only show up while he walks over to use them. The DJ deck, couch, bed, arcade, desk, butterfly, gym, fridge, plant and easel need Wander or Free roam. Sign, marble race and goodbye are manual: they only happen when you press Do it, unless a stream mode plays them (Starting soon and BRB hold a sign, Marble race cheers, Ending waves goodbye).
Starting soon and BRB hold up a sign first. Gaming keeps him at his desk, marble keeps him cheering. Party brings out a disco ball and makes him busier. Ending waves, bows and walks off.
